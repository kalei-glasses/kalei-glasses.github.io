Privacy notice

Who is responsible for your data

ASSOCIATION JUNIOR ENTERPRISE EACH, with the details above. For anything about your data, write to kalei.glasses@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the sign-up form: your email, your name (optional), your country, the version you are interested in, and, if you sign up for a child, an age range. We do not collect your child's name or any other data about them.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To send you information about Kalei and, if you ticked the box, to tell you when early access opens.
  • To count, in anonymous totals, how much interest there is in each version. This is part of validating our student project.

We do this because you agreed to it when you signed up (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us, and we will delete your data.

Who processes it

Your answers are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets (Google Ireland Limited). Google may process data in the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Only the Kalei team at EACH can see them. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Kalei is expected to move from EACH to its own company in the future. If that happens, we will tell you by email before your data is passed on, and you will be able to unsubscribe first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit how we use it, to receive a copy of it, or to object to its use. Write to kalei.glasses@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es) or the authority where you live.

Hosting, fonts and your browser

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it receives your IP address when you visit. The fonts are stored on this website, so your browser does not connect to Google or any other third party to load them. We do not use cookies or tracking. Your choices on the page (language, font, kids or adults) are saved only in your own browser and never sent to us.
